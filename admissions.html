Admissions for Session 2026–27

Join a distinguished community of scholars committed to academic brilliance, scientific innovation, and moral integrity.

Straightforward Process

4 Simple Steps to Enrollment

We maintain a transparent and merit-based admission procedure guided by state educational regulations.

1

Online Enquiry

Fill the online enquiry form below or collect the prospectus booklet from the campus admissions office.

2

Document Check

Submit previous grade marksheets, Transfer Certificate (TC), Aadhaar card, and passport-size photographs.

3

Interaction / Test

Short interaction and foundational assessment to ascertain subject aptitude (especially for 11th Science/Commerce).

4

Seat Confirmation

Remit institutional admission dues, obtain student ID card, and receive official academic timetable & book list.

Standard Benchmarks

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must fulfill the minimum qualification criteria stipulated by the board for admission into respective streams.

Class / Stream Eligibility Benchmark
Class IX (9th) Passed Class VIII from any recognized board/school with valid TC.
Class XI (Science PCM) Passed High School (10th) with min. 60% aggregate and 60%+ in Math & Science.
Class XI (Science PCB) Passed High School (10th) with min. 55% aggregate and 55%+ in Science.
Class XI (Commerce) Passed High School (10th) with min. 50% aggregate across all subjects.
Class XI (Humanities/Arts) Passed High School (10th) examination from recognized state/central board.
Verification Dossier

Required Documents

Please keep original certificates and 2 sets of self-attested photocopies ready for registration.

  • Original Transfer Certificate (TC) counter-signed by education authority.
  • Marksheet of Previous Qualifying Examination (Class 8th / 10th).
  • Character & Conduct Certificate from the last attended school.
  • Copy of Student & Parents' Aadhaar Card (Identity proof).
  • Six recent colored passport-size photographs of the student.
  • Caste / Income Certificate (If seeking state scholarship benefit).

Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding admission timelines, fee concessions, and board transfers.

What is the last date to submit admission forms for session 2026–27?
The first round of online and offline registration typically remains open until June 30th. However, seats in Science streams (PCM/PCB) are allotted on a first-come, first-served merit basis.
Are scholarship concessions available for meritorious and needy students?
Yes! Students scoring 85%+ in their previous board examinations receive merit fee waivers. Government SC/ST/OBC and EWS scholarship schemes are fully facilitated through our administrative counter.
Can students from CBSE / ICSE boards apply for Class 11th admission?
Absolutely. Students migrating from CBSE, ICSE, or other state education boards are welcome upon submitting their Migration Certificate and counter-signed Transfer Certificate.
