Institution Overview

Rooted in Tradition, Reaching for the Future

S. N. Patel Inter College was founded in 1994 by eminent educationists and philanthropists who envisioned an academic haven where rural and urban scholars could access world-class schooling without barriers.

Over three decades of dedication have transformed this campus into an academic cornerstone in Uttar Pradesh, recognized by the Department of Education and affiliated with the State Board. Today, it accommodates over 2,800 students under the guidance of over 85 dedicated educators.

Chronicles of Progress

Milestones in Our Journey

Key moments that shaped S. N. Patel Inter College from a humble academy into a premier higher secondary institution.

1994

Foundation & Inception

Inaugurated with 150 students and 8 teachers with the noble vision of democratizing quality science and arts education in the region.

2004

Upgradation to Intermediate (10+2) College

Formal state recognition granted for Intermediate Science (PCM/PCB) and Commerce streams alongside new laboratories and central library expansion.

2015

Smart Campus & Computer Wing Launch

Installation of digital audio-visual lecture rooms, high-speed computer network labs, and modern sports grounds.

2026

32+ Years of Unbroken Educational Brilliance

Now serving 2,800+ scholars with unbroken record of 99%+ board pass percentages and countless alumni succeeding across civil services, defense, medicine, and engineering.
