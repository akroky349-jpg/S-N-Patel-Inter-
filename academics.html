Academic Programs & Curriculum

Structured pedagogical paths designed to instill deep conceptual mastery, analytical rigor, and exceptional performance in board and competitive examinations.

Comprehensive Education

The Academic Structure

We offer secondary and senior secondary programs recognized by the Uttar Pradesh Board of High School and Intermediate Education.

Secondary (IX & X)

Solid foundational grounding in languages, mathematics, sciences, and social sciences with continuous comprehensive evaluation.

Senior Secondary (XI & XII)

Choice of Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce, and Humanities streams mentored by specialized subject masters.

Competitive Foundation

Integrated coaching guidance for JEE Main, NEET, CUET, NDA, and civil entrance foundations alongside board syllabus.

Grades IX & X

Secondary High School Curriculum

Focusing on deep conceptual clarity, logical thinking, and language command preparing scholars for the High School Board Examination.

Compulsory Subjects

  • Hindi (General / Elementary): Literature, grammar, and formal composition.
  • English Language & Literature: Reading comprehension, grammar, and writing skills.
  • Mathematics: Algebra, geometry, trigonometry, mensuration, and statistics.
  • Science: Integrated Physics, Chemistry, and Biology with mandatory lab work.
  • Social Science: History, Geography, Civics, and Economics.

Elective & Co-Curricular

  • Computer Science / Information Technology: Programming basics, office productivity.
  • Drawing & Fine Arts: Freehand sketching, watercolor, and design.
  • Sanskrit (Optional Classical Language): Grammar, shlokas, and cultural texts.
  • Physical & Health Education: Regular athletics, yoga, and sports evaluation.
  • Moral Science & Community Work: Value-oriented civic service projects.
Grades XI & XII

Senior Secondary — Science Stream (PCM / PCB)

Tailored for students aspiring to pursue Engineering, Medicine, Pure Sciences, Biotechnology, Architecture, and Research.

MATHEMATICS GROUP (PCM)

Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics

Core focus on mathematical modeling, mechanics, electromagnetism, and chemical kinetics.

  • • Advanced Physics & Mechanics Laboratory
  • • Organic, Inorganic & Physical Chemistry
  • • Calculus, Coordinate Geometry & Vectors
  • • General English & General Hindi
  • • Optional: Computer Science / Physical Education
BIOLOGY GROUP (PCB)

Physics, Chemistry & Biology

Ideal for medical aspirants with microscopic specimen study, botanical taxonomy, and physiology.

  • • Botany & Zoology Hands-on Practical Labs
  • • Human Anatomy, Genetics & Ecology
  • • Physical & Applied Chemistry
  • • General English & General Hindi
  • • Optional: Computer Science / Physical Education
Grades XI & XII

Senior Secondary — Commerce & Humanities (Arts)

Designed for future chartered accountants, corporate executives, civil servants, legal scholars, and journalists.

COMMERCE STREAM

Business & Financial Studies

  • • Accountancy: Double-entry bookkeeping, corporate and partnership accounts.
  • • Business Studies: Management principles, finance, and marketing.
  • • Economics: Microeconomics, Macroeconomics, and Indian Economic Development.
  • • Commercial Mathematics / Informatics Practices: Business stats & software.
  • • General Hindi & General English: Business correspondence & literature.
HUMANITIES & ARTS STREAM

Liberal Arts & Social Sciences

  • • History: Ancient, Medieval, and Modern Indian & World civilizations.
  • • Geography: Physical geography, resource management, and cartography.
  • • Political Science: Indian Constitution, governance, and political theories.
  • • Sociology / Psychology: Social institutions, culture, and human behavior.
  • • Literature: Advanced Hindi & English literary studies.
Pedagogical Excellence

Our Teaching Methodology

A student-centric learning model that replaces rote memorization with conceptual inquiry, practical application, and mentorship.

Smart Interactive Lectures

Audiovisual 3D models and digital presentations that make complex physics, biology, and mathematical proofs clear and engaging.

Mandatory Lab Practicals

Hands-on experimentation twice every week for all science subjects to connect theory with tangible empirical observations.

Continuous Assessments

Fortnightly unit tests, monthly assessments, and mock board exams with detailed question-by-question feedback for students.

Remedial Mentorship

Dedicated zero-period doubt sessions every afternoon for students needing additional clarification and personalized care.

Career Counseling Cell

Regular career seminars with invited university professors, IITians, doctors, and civil servants to guide post-12th aspirations.

Board Exam Bootcamps

Specialized answer-writing workshops, previous 10-year question solving marathons, and time-management simulations.
