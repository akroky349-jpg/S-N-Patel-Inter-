Visitor Guidelines

How to Reach Our Campus

S. N. Patel Inter College is centrally located with exceptional public and private connectivity.

By Train (Railways)

Nearest major railway station is situated just 2.5 km from the college entrance with regular auto and e-rickshaw shuttles.

By Bus (State Roadways)

The district roadways bus terminal is located 1.8 km away. College shuttle bus service also runs daily routes.

Security & Gate Passes

Visitors must register at Main Security Gate 1 with valid photo ID before proceeding to the administrative building.
