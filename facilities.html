Scientific Discovery

Specialized Science Laboratories

Hands-on empirical science is the lifeblood of our curriculum. S. N. Patel Inter College hosts three independent, spacious, and safety-certified laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.

Physics Laboratory: Optical benches, galvanometers, sonometers, prism spectrometers, and electrical circuit kits conforming to board practicals.
Chemistry Laboratory: Acid-resistant workstations, high-precision digital balances, titration apparatus, fume safety hoods, and eye-wash safety points.
Biology & Life Sciences Laboratory: High-magnification compound microscopes, preserved anatomical specimens, 3D anatomical charts, and botanical slides.
3
Dedicated Fully-Equipped Science Laboratories

Physical Fitness

Sports Complex & Athletic Fields

Physical vigor and teamwork are indispensable to education. Our expansive campus features dedicated sports facilities overseen by qualified Physical Education directors.

Standard Cricket Pitch & Football Arena: Full-sized grounds hosting inter-school tournaments, annual sports meets, and drill displays.
Volleyball & Badminton Courts: Dedicated all-weather floodlit courts with professional net systems and safety surfaces.
Indoor Sports & Yoga Pavilion: Table Tennis, Chess, Carrom, and daily morning Yoga / Pranayama sessions for mental equilibrium.

Acres of Open Athletic Playing Fields
Comfort & Well-being

Essential Campus Amenities

Prioritizing health, security, seamless transport, and modern presentation technologies across the college.

Smart Digital Classrooms

Interactive audiovisual boards, high-definition digital projectors, and digital textbook integrations in every lecture room.

24x7 CCTV & Security

Comprehensive surveillance across corridors, gates, and perimeters manned by vigilant security personnel at all times.

College Bus Transport

A fleet of well-maintained school buses connecting neighboring towns and rural routes, equipped with GPS and first-aid kits.

Medical Room & First Aid

Dedicated wellness infirmary with on-call physician, registered nurse, emergency oxygen, and basic health supplies.

RO Purified Water

Multi-stage reverse osmosis water filtration coolers across every floor ensuring pristine, hygienic drinking water.

Solar & Green Energy

Rooftop solar panel installations delivering clean backup power, paired with rainwater harvesting and lush botanical flora.
