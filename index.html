Welcome to S. N. Patel Inter College

A Prestigious Seat of Learning Built on Discipline & Wisdom

Established with a noble mission to democratize quality education, S. N. Patel Inter College stands as a landmark institution dedicated to intellectual curiosity, moral fortitude, and scientific temper.

We offer rigorous instruction in High School (9th–10th) and Intermediate (11th–12th) across Science (Mathematics & Biology), Commerce, and Arts streams, guided by a distinguished faculty and state-of-the-art infrastructure.

Holistic Moral & Physical Development

Expansive athletics fields, NCC/Scouts, and vibrant cultural societies alongside academics.

Modern Smart Classrooms & Laboratories

Guiding Philosophy

Our Vision, Mission & Academic Tenets

Guided by the sacred proverb "Vidya Dadati Vinayam" (Knowledge Bestows Humility), our institution molds students into ethical, skilled, and patriotic citizens.

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive, equitable, and modern education to students from all walks of life. We cultivate critical reasoning, scientific exploration, communicative poise, and compassionate leadership through disciplined academic mentoring.

  • Student-centric experiential teaching methods.
  • Personalized guidance and remedial support for every learner.
  • Cultivating respect for Indian heritage and cultural values.

Our Vision

To be recognized as a benchmark institution of regional and national repute that inspires academic excellence, empowers rural and urban youth alike, and prepares confident scholars who excel in higher universities and competitive careers.

  • 100% university and competitive examination readiness.
  • State-of-the-art technological adoption in every syllabus.
  • Lifelong passion for inquiry, service, and civic responsibility.

Dr. R. K. Patel

M.Sc., M.Ed., Ph.D. • Principal
